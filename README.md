The note: **"Door log: 9 opens since midnight, all to stare at a yogurt that expired Tuesday. It has not changed. I checked."** (111 characters, one line.)

The fridge speaks like a smug appliance with receipts: a door log, an inventory, and a yogurt that expired Tuesday. The sting is the fridge confirming from its own sensors that nine rounds of staring changed nothing.
